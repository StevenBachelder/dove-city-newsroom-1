LEVEL I — The Classic Six

Welcome back to the desk. You survived the tip queue; now you learn the names. Before anyone diagnoses a living operation, they learn anatomy — clean specimens, one technique at a time, from the field's classic chart.

Three rooms, each with less scaffolding than the last:

1.a The Introduction — three plates per technique, in order. The structure itself is your tutor.
1.b Last One Standing — one of each, shuffled; every correct name removes its button. Elimination is a diagnostic skill too.
1.c The Scramble — shuffled, with extras mixed in. The structure goes silent; only the content speaks.

Term I is the classroom: no doors here. The doors return in Term II — the archive deserves seriousness.
