Research use & foundations

Using the game in studies

A pre/post discernment instrument (two parallel 8-item forms in the game's tip format) accompanies the project. For consenting classes, adding ?research=1 to the game address shows a "Research log (JSON)" button at the debrief: one click downloads an anonymous file of decisions (technique, level, verdict, correctness, tool use, pins, timestamps). No names, no accounts; nothing leaves the device without the student clicking. Follow institutional ethics procedures.

Foundations

Lateral reading: Wineburg & McGrew (2019). Technique inoculation through play: Roozenbeek & van der Linden (2019); classroom evidence with teens: Axelsson et al. (2024). Learning design: Gee (2003; 2005); Vygotsky. Information influence & resilience: Falkheimer & Pamment (2026, MPF). Identity-targeted disinformation: Hedling (2025). Campaign anatomy: Ranstorp & Ahlerup (2023). Threat context: Säkerhetspolisen Lägesbild 2025–2026. Hate-speech boundary reference: Siegel (2020).
