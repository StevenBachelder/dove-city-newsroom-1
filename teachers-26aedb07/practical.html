Differentiation & troubleshooting

Differentiation

  • Struggling readers: pairs, "read aloud, decide together."
  • Fast finishers: replay for the Master Badge — or get politely fired ON PURPOSE and explain every mistake (surprisingly instructive).
  • Motion sensitivity: system "reduce motion" is respected automatically.
  • Language teaching: play in the target language, debrief in the classroom language.

Troubleshooting

  • Odd layout/old version → Ctrl+Shift+R (Cmd+Shift+R on Mac).
  • Small placeholder where a picture should be → note the shown file path, report to the team.
  • No sound → mute switch in top bar, then device volume; sound starts after first click.
  • Closed tab mid-game → no save by design; a rerun takes 20 min and repetition is fine.
