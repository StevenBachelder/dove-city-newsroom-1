Technique glossary

BadgePlain-language meaning
ManipulatedThe picture was edited — or entirely made by AI.
Out of contextReal picture, wrong story. Check where and when it FIRST appeared.
ConspiracyA hidden-hand explanation glued onto ordinary footage.
Unverified"Sources say" with no source you can check. A claim is not a fact.
PolarizationAn enemy written into a harmless scene: us versus them.
FabricatedThe event never happened at all (the BAD DOG "death").
Emotion ≠ falseScary and sad can still be TRUE. Feelings say check, not reject.
Strategic narrativePosts as building blocks of a constructed larger story — flattering its sender, attacking a target, or deflecting with humor and memes. Ask what story a post serves.
Liar's dividendWhen anything could be AI-made, denying REAL evidence gets easier. The answer is provenance: originals, first appearance, documented context.
Ad-SpotterSearch engines sell the top of the page. Ads dressed as results.
