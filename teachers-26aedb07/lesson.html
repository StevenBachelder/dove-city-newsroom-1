Running the lesson

Intro (5–10 min)

Ask how professionals check things online; draw out the key move — they leave the page. Name it: lateral reading. Announce the newsroom standard: at most one mistake per level.

Play (25–40 min)

Students play through the debrief. Point them at the three emblem guides and the taped checklist. Do not rescue them from level restarts — the retry is the mastery mechanic and the level report names the technique that won.

Debrief questions (10–15 min)

  • Which badges did you earn — and what does a grey card's back teach?
  • Which tip fooled you; what evidence would have saved you?
  • When did the absence of coverage prove something? ("Silence is a clue.")
  • Footballer case: "a test happened" vs "the test was positive" — what's the difference?
  • Convoy case: a two-year-old ad became tonight's invasion rumour. Who profits from your click?
  • Storming case: the photo was real — so what was the lie? (The crop.)
  • When did a story make you FEEL strongly — and what does the game call your feelings? (The target.)

The take-home

Each student can download an Applicant Report at the debrief — ledger, badges with explanations, improvement areas. Use as exit ticket or reflection basis ("explain one badge you did NOT earn, in your own words").
