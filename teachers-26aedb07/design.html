The learning design — teaching WITH it

The structure follows Gee's principles of learning in good games:

  • Identity: a trial applicant with a press card, judged by a named editor. Let the fiction stand — it's doing motivational work.
  • Safe risk: retries exist so students can be brave; a failed level restores everything and names the winning technique. Hence: don't rescue.
  • Cycles of expertise: techniques return harder (crude edit → AI image). Level-3 struggle is deliberate destabilization, not regression.
  • Just-in-time: nothing is front-loaded on purpose; guides open when wanted, the checklist sits at the decision. Resist pre-teaching.
  • Situated vocabulary: concepts mean their case first, their definition second. Debrief move: "explain the badge USING its case" — the transfer moment.
  • Performance before competence: stamping starts at tip one; competence grows out of scaffolded doing — why pair-talk is valuable.
