# City Newsroom — Pre/Post Discernment Instrument (v1 draft)

Mirrors Axelsson et al. (2024): technique-level discernment measured with held-out items in the game's own tip format. Administer PRE (before first play) and POST (after Level 3 / debrief). ~8 min each. Items are NOT in the game deck.

**Format per item:** show a tip card (headline · sender · one line of body · buzz value). Participant answers: (a) Publish / Flag as false, (b) confidence 1–5, (c) open text: "What made you decide?"

**Scoring:** discernment = correct(a); technique recognition coded from (c) against the badge taxonomy; confidence calibration = (b) vs correctness.

## Form A (PRE) — 8 items
1. "SCHOOL LUNCH SECRETLY CONTAINS INSECT FLOUR — leaked memo!" · @truthparent22 · "They never asked us. Share before it's deleted!" — *fake / conspiracy framing + unverified*
2. "City marathon rerouted after water-main burst on Mill Street" · City News traffic desk · official detour map attached — *true / genuine*
3. "Photo: riot outside the youth centre last night" · @citywatchreal · tightly cropped image, three people visible — *fake / out-of-context (photo is from a concert queue)*
4. "Pop star cancels tour: 'complete exhaustion', label confirms" · GNN Entertainment · statement quoted, dated today — *true / genuine (emotionally big but confirmed)*
5. "AI catches teacher grading unfairly — screenshot proof" · @justice4students · screenshot of a chat, no source app visible — *fake / fabricated + unverified*
6. "New skate park opens Saturday — first 100 get free helmets" · SPONSORED · looks like news, small 'ad' tag — *ad recognition item*
7. "THEY are bussing in outsiders to 'clean up' our neighbourhood" · @realvoices_north · night photo of two buses — *fake / polarization (us-vs-them)*
8. "Flood warning: river expected to peak at 02:00, low streets closing" · City Rescue Service · alarming tone, official sender — *true / emotion ≠ false*

## Form B (POST) — 8 parallel items
Same technique slots, new surface content (1: gym "hidden cameras" memo; 2: library opening hours; 3: cropped 'fight' = dance battle; 4: athlete retirement confirmed; 5: 'leaked' grade list screenshot; 6: energy-drink advertorial; 7: 'they are taking our practice field'; 8: storm-surge warning).

**Counterbalance** A/B order across classes. Retain item wording exactly across conditions; translate ×4 with the youth panel before deployment.
