# Draft case: "They're taking the pitch" (identity-targeted, per Hedling 2025) — FOR YOUTH-PANEL REVIEW, not yet in deck

**Design intent:** one case where the targeted identity is plausibly the *player's own* (local youth football culture), so students feel the pull Hedling describes — belonging, not ignorance, as the vulnerability.

**Tip (Level 2, fake, technique=polarization):** "CONFIRMED: council giving OUR training pitch to 'event partners' — youth teams locked out from Monday" · sender @northside_ultras_real · photo: padlocked gate, evening. Buzz 14.

**Truth:** the gate photo is real — from last winter's routine re-turfing closure. The council calendar shows youth training unchanged; the 'event partner' is a weekend veterans' tournament sharing Saturday mornings only.

**Evidence set (sketch):** [+] Council facilities calendar (training times unchanged, dated) · [+] Club's own post from last winter with the same padlock photo ("re-turfing week!") · [ ] angry reply threads · [ ] "insider" anonymous account · [AD] sports-shop 'petition' page collecting emails.

**Pedagogy:** the anger is *reasonable if true* — the lesson is checking precisely when the story is about *your* people and *your* place. Feedback line: "The photo was yours all along — last winter's. When a story is about your own team, your feelings are the crop."

**Panel questions:** Does the scenario read as real to you? Is the sender name plausible? What detail would make YOU share it before checking? What wording is off?
