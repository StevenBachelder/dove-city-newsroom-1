Boilerplate

City Newsroom is a free browser game that puts teenagers at the tip desk of a newsroom attacked the day before — and asks them to decide, tip by tip, what is true enough to publish. Built on verification research and classroom-tested learning science, it teaches lateral reading and eight manipulation techniques in under 40 minutes, in four languages.

Key facts

• Free, browser-based, no accounts · • EN/SV/FI/ET · • 3 levels, 15 cases, 9 badges · • One-mistake editorial standard with a politely firm ending · • Built-in classroom instruments and research logging · • Level 2 of the SOURCE trilogy
