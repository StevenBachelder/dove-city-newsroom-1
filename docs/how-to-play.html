How to play

You are a trial applicant at the tip desk. Tips come in; you decide what goes out — in print and on air.

The loop

Professional fact-checkers don't stay on the page they're judging — they leave it at once to see what other sources say about the sender and the claim. That is lateral reading, and ordinary "vertical" reading is how people get fooled. So: open a tip from Incoming Tips, then investigate with Text Search (where did the claim first appear, and how has it been reported?) and Reverse Image Search — the truth is never inside the tip itself. Pin your strongest evidence to the board (three pins max). Then stamp: PUBLISH or FLAG AS FALSE.

The standard

Three levels, five tips each, and the fakes stop looking fake. One wrong stamp per level is tolerated; a second restarts the level — same tips, fresh eyes, everything restored. You have three do-overs in total. After a fourth failed level, Sarah shows you the door. Politely.

Both errors count

Publishing a fake damages every reader who trusts you. But flagging a true story is also a mistake — emotion ≠ false, and the scoop won't wait. There is no safe option, just good judgement.

Help on the desk

And always weigh the sender: who is behind this content — what is their purpose, and who funds them?
