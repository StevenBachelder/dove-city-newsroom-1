# Field Orientation — where our taxonomy sits in the larger landscape
*Due-diligence comparison against the major frameworks, ancient and modern. Companion to MASTER_TAXONOMY.md.*
*NB: framework version details should be confirmed with the Uppsala/Cambridge partners before citation.*

## A. The landscape at three altitudes

**Operational catalogs (the comprehensive lists):**
- **DISARM** (ex-AMITT): the field's MITRE-style open framework — 100+ techniques on a kill chain (plan → develop → legitimize → amplify → persist). The due-diligence backstop; any technique we mint should be checked against it.
- **ABCDE** (Actors, Behavior, Content, Degree, Effect) and **FIMI** (EU/EEAS): analytic frames for whole operations rather than single techniques.
- **Wardle & Derakhshan**: mis/dis/mal-information + seven content types — the canonical typology; source of our malinformation gap-find.

**Strategic move sets (the "moves behind the moves"):**
- **Nimmo's 4D**: *dismiss, distort, distract, dismay* — the compact modern answer to deflection/confusion/smokescreen.
- **Carley's BEND**: 16 maneuvers in four quadrants — constructive vs. destructive, aimed at *narratives* vs. at *communities*. Maps directly onto the designer's world-building / worldview-erosion axis.
- **RAND's firehose**; Pomerantsev's *censorship through noise*; the influence-operations kill chain (Schneier).

**Rhetorical/cognitive traditions (the deep lineage):**
- **FLICC** (Cook): Fake experts, Logical fallacies, Impossible expectations, Cherry-picking, Conspiracy — expanded to a ~30-node tree; basis of **Cranky Uncle**, the third inoculation-game lineage (add to our Bad News / Harmony Square comparison set).
- **IPA 1937 seven devices**: name-calling, glittering generalities, transfer, testimonial, plain folks, card-stacking, bandwagon.
- **Schopenhauer's 38 stratagems** (*The Art of Being Right*): eristic moves for victory regardless of truth — the Smuggled Premise's family album.
- **The Greeks**: Aristotle's *Sophistical Refutations* (thirteen fallacies — the first technique list); ethos/pathos/logos as the attack surfaces; **kairos** — timing as rhetorical resource, the ancient name for the Gallop's advantage; the sophists as the first professional operators.

## B. Cross-check: our 14 + tiers against the field

**Well-covered by us:** manipulation of material (stronger than any of the game lineages), sender identity (impersonation → cloned outlets), amplification family, credibility warfare (discredit/denialism/insinuation ≈ Nimmo's *dismiss*), conspiracy, polarization, emotional axis with the emotional-true guard-rail (our distinctive contribution — no other framework carries the inoculation-against-the-inoculation).

**Named elsewhere, absorbed by ours:** card-stacking = cherry-picking (Tier 1 ✓); FLICC's fake-experts ≈ impersonation + astroturf; distort ≈ manipulated/out-of-context; Schopenhauer's extension/homonymy tricks ≈ Smuggled Premise cousins.

**Genuine gaps the comparison exposes (candidates for the Conversations channel):**
1. **Bandwagon / manufactured social proof** as a *rhetorical* move distinct from technical amplification — "everyone already believes this" said in words, not bots.
2. **Glittering generalities & transfer** — virtue-words and borrowed prestige ("For Our Public Safety" is transfer; we enact it but don't name it).
3. **Testimonial / fake authority voice** — adjacent to impersonation but rhetorically distinct (real celebrity, irrelevant authority).
4. **Distraction proper** (Nimmo's third D) — the dead cat, the smokescreen; whataboutism is one species of a larger genus we should name at genus level.
5. **Dismay / demoralization — worldview erosion as the goal.** The designer's phrase names what Pomerantsev and Arendt described: operations that seek not belief but exhaustion — *"nothing is true and everything is possible."* Cynicism induction is the terminal technique; our denialism badge is its retail form, but the strategic version (making verification feel pointless) deserves its own entry. Arguably the deepest threat a verification game exists to answer.
6. **Kairos attacks** — timing as the weapon (pre-planted alibis and the Gallop are instances; election-eve leaks the archetype).

## C. The orienting compass (proposal)

Two axes hold all of it, and match the designer's framing:
- **Constructive ↔ Destructive**: building a world-view vs. eroding one (BEND's split).
- **Against the story ↔ Against the trust-system**: attacking what people believe vs. attacking their *capacity and will* to verify.
Our five families, the 4Ds, FLICC, and the Greek canon all place cleanly on this plane — and the game's deepest purpose locates in the corner the frameworks least serve: **defending the trust-system itself against erosion.** City News doesn't only teach players to spot lies; it rehearses the conviction that checking is worth it. That is the anti-dismay position, and it may be the project's truest north.

## D. Due-diligence protocol going forward
Every Conversations-channel entry and every minted badge gets a one-line check against: DISARM (does it exist there? under what name?), FLICC, IPA-7, Nimmo-4D, and Schopenhauer. Five lookups, five minutes, and our taxonomy stays honest to the field while keeping its own voice.
