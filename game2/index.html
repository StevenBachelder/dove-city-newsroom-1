LEVEL II — The Full Chart

Most of the city is real. Your job is precision, not suspicion. Five of these fifteen tips are genuine — "genuine" is always on the chart, and choosing it correctly is as skilled as any diagnosis.

These are the fifteen tips from your first days on the desk — now you name what was load-bearing in each. Several things may be present; one carries the story.

Each tip is handled in three deductive steps — you never weigh everything at once:

1. What do you see? Select every property present in the tip.
2. Sort what you found — each property is a MEANS (how it was done), an AIM (what it serves), or a STATE (what's true).
3. What carries the story? Pick the load-bearing property — from your own findings only.

After each correct naming, mark your confidence (1–3 pins). And the doors are back: four first-try misses close the level — the archive deserves seriousness. You can always re-enter.
