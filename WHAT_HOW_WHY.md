# The Three-Axis Turn — What, How, Why
*A disruption, welcomed. Designer's discovery, Aug 2026; the record prepares decisions, it does not make them.*

## 1. The disruption (designer's words, preserved)
The existing games — Bad News, and our own work equally — inherit a paradigm from before information became ubiquitously accessible, and under it they treat disinformation phenomena "as a single solidified phenomena, as more of a What than a How and a Why." Sometimes a tag names the How ("manipulation"), sometimes the Why ("division") — e.g. the immigrants-in-the-forest case. The missing layer is "the more granular articulation that looks at both HOW and WHY as well as the synthesised end result. The means to the ends. A falsified fire (falsification) for a xenophobic attack... or perhaps some part of a coordinated effort towards... an envisioned division towards a mono-culture world-building which would include and lift some while exclude and disempower others."

And the pedagogical covenant: starting fused is legitimate — "we must meet the player where they stand. But it is our responsibility not to leave them there. We must take them for an interesting, fun and insightful walk-and-talk through the forest (the ZPD we offer), and leave them in a completely new position with a better view... and give them both the understanding and the tools of experiential knowledge of how they got there."

## 2. What is already known (the field, mapped onto the turn)
- **Ends / Ways / Means** (strategy doctrine) and **Aristotle's four causes**: material (the doctored image), efficient (the production technique), formal (the genre — "breaking news alert"), final (division). What/How/Why is the four causes, operationalized.
- **ABC(DE)** (François): Actors / Behavior / Content — built precisely because "disinformation" conflated these; our turn is the same separation at tag level.
- **DISARM's kill chain** separates phases of How; **Nimmo's 4Ds** are mid-level Ends; **Wardle & Derakhshan's mis/dis/mal** is keyed on intent — a Why-axis the technique lists never absorbed.
- **Our five families** ("what the technique attacks") is a fourth axis already in the record: the **surface**. Neither means nor end — the target.
- The **attribution problem**: professional analysis treats intent with confidence levels and pattern evidence, never single-artifact certainty. No inoculation game currently teaches this. Ours will.

## 3. What this project has (unknowingly) already built toward
Game 1 = **provenance** (the layer that survives cheap fabrication). Game 2 = **anatomy of means** (checkable properties). The investigator role the designer foreshadows = **ends-analysis** — and its instrument already exists: the sender census, track records, and Case Ledger, because intent is invisible in one post but *legible in a repertoire*. The three games are the three axes. The paradigm critique names what the architecture was already doing.

## 4. DECIDED (as of this document)
1. **Faceted, not Linnaean.** No single genus-species tree across axes. Ground truth stays flat multi-tags (`techniques:[]`) = theory-neutral observations; interpretation lives in a separate, revisable **axis map** (axis_map.js): each slug declared MEANS / END / FORM / STATE, with `disputed` flags where honest. Re-interpret forever; re-tag never.
2. **Tags are criterial properties.** A tag must be checkable against the artifact (inter-rater reliable). Effects and intents are not tags; they are readings.
3. **Never grade a Why as a fact.** Ends-claims are scored on the case made — evidence cited, cross-post patterns shown, confidence marked — not on matching a hidden answer. (The confidence-pin mechanic and the Ledger are the machinery for this.)
4. **The observability gradient sequences the curriculum.** L1–L2: means and surfaces (checkable). Investigator level: ends — speculative by nature, therefore taught *as disciplined speculation*, the way analysts actually work.
5. **L2 stays as built** (naming the load-bearing tag) but its feedback may begin articulating the axes: "manipulated (the means) serving division (the end)" — the walk-and-talk starts mid-forest, not at the summit.

## 5. OPEN (explicitly undecided; do not back into these)
- Criterial checklists per tag (what exactly qualifies a post as `astroturf`?) — needed before any inter-rater study.
- Where FORM tags (conspiracy-as-genre) finally live; whether prenarration is means or form.
- Whether ENDS belong in ground-truth data at all, or only ever in the investigator's argued readings. (Current lean: the latter, with a small set of *candidate-end* labels the investigator chooses among.)
- The single-word problem: when one answer must be chosen (L2), which axis wins? Current answer: the axis the level teaches — L2 asks for the load-bearing *observable*. Revisit after playtests.
- Whether the Why-layer needs its own vocabulary (division, dismay, profit, capture, erasure...) distinct from technique slugs. (Likely yes; Nimmo's Ds + the charter's erosion language are the seed.)

## 5b. The Russian-doll refinement (designer’s correction, and what it clarified)
“Fusion dolls” was a typo for **Russian dolls** — and the correction sharpens the architecture into three distinct structures with three distinct jobs:
- **Facets** classify a post’s *properties* (means, form, state): flat, orthogonal, never nested. The tag system.
- **Dolls** nest *purposes*: falsified fire ⊂ xenophobic attack ⊂ exclusionary world-building. Nesting is true of ENDS, false of CATEGORIES. The investigator’s work is opening the dolls — post → operation → campaign → project — each shell opened only with repertoire evidence and a confidence pin. (Game 3’s central gesture, named by accident.)
- **Phylogeny** (the Darwinian reading) traces a technique’s *descent*: trolling’s historical umbrella splitting into engagement-bait + polarization is already taught as “names evolve; the field is alive.” A genealogy of techniques is legitimate scholarship and a legitimate lesson — but it is history, not classification.
Conflating any two of these is the corner. Keeping all three, each in its lane, is the map.

## 6. The corner-avoidance principles
Data/theory separation (facets over trees) · properties vs. readings · confidence over verdicts for intent · vocabulary layers versioned independently · and the covenant: fused categories are a *starting position we owe the player an exit from* — the hill, the view, and the knowledge of the path.
