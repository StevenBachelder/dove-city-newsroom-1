# SOURCE — Reading List for the Three-Axis Work
*Annotated, ordered, and armed with a method. "Humans are slow" — but slow reading with the right instrument is research.*

## THE METHOD (do this while reading, and the reading becomes a paper)
**The lane audit.** For every taxonomy you read, keep one worksheet: list every named item, and mark it **M / E / F / S** (means, end, form, state) — plus **P** where a placement is genuinely disputed. When you're done, you will hold a cross-framework lane census: the empirical demonstration that the whole field mixes kinds, with numbers. That figure — "lane composition of nine major taxonomies" — is publishable on its own, and no one has made it. Your reading time converts directly into the turn's strongest evidence.

## PHASE 1 — Short primary documents (one sitting each; start here)
1. **Wardle & Derakhshan, *Information Disorder* (Council of Europe report, 2017).** The canonical grid: mis/dis/mal keyed on falseness × harm — the field's one existing INTENT axis. Audit question: which of their seven types are means, which are states?
2. **Camille François, *Actors, Behaviors, Content: A Disinformation ABC* (2019).** Built precisely because "disinformation" conflated kinds — the closest existing ancestor of the turn. Short memo.
3. **Ben Nimmo, the 4Ds** (*Anatomy of an Info-War*, 2015) **+ *The Breakout Scale*** (Brookings, 2020). Dismiss/distort/distract/dismay = pure ENDS vocabulary; Breakout = a measurement scale, a different kind of thing again.
4. **Paul & Matthews, *The Russian "Firehose of Falsehood"* (RAND, 2016).** Eight pages; volume-and-tempo as strategy. The Gallop's institutional cousin.
5. **Aristotle, *Sophistical Refutations*.** The first technique list (thirteen fallacies), ~40 pages. Audit it like the moderns — it sorts surprisingly cleanly, which is itself a finding.
6. **Schopenhauer, *The Art of Being Right* (38 stratagems).** An ENDS-first taxonomy (winning, regardless of truth) — the mirror image of DISARM's means-first list. The Smuggled Premise's family album.

## PHASE 2 — The game lineages (your collaborators' spine)
7. **Roozenbeek & van der Linden, "The fake news game" (2019)** + **Basol et al. (2020)** — the inoculation evidence base behind Bad News's six.
8. **Roozenbeek & van der Linden, Harmony Square paper** (HKS Misinformation Review, 2020).
9. **Cook, Ellerton & Kinkead, "Deconstructing climate misinformation" (2018)** + the **FLICC taxonomy** (skepticalscience's expanded tree) + **Cranky Uncle** (play it). The third lineage; FLICC's tree structure is the closest thing to a genus-species attempt — audit where it strains.
10. **van der Linden, *Foolproof* (2023).** The accessible synthesis; useful for shared vocabulary with Cambridge.

## PHASE 3 — The operational frameworks (slow, reference-style reading)
11. **DISARM** (disarm.foundation — the Red framework online). 100+ TTPs on a kill chain. Don't read linearly; audit by phase. Note: it is means-almost-all-the-way-down — the census will show it.
12. **Carley, "Social cybersecurity" (2020) + the BEND papers.** Sixteen maneuvers in four quadrants — the only framework already split constructive/destructive × narrative/community. Closest cousin to your world-building/erosion axis.
13. **EEAS FIMI Threat Landscape Reports (1st & 2nd).** How institutions operationalize all of this today; FIMI's behaviour-first definition is ABC's institutional child.

## PHASE 4 — The deep layer (the "larger than the game" shelf)
14. **Jacques Ellul, *Propaganda* (1965).** The classic argument that propaganda is an *environment*, not a message — the philosophical ancestor of your worldview-building frame.
15. **Hannah Arendt, "Truth and Politics"** (essay) — organized lying and manufactured cynicism; the intellectual root of the anti-dismay position.
16. **Peter Pomerantsev, *This Is Not Propaganda* (2019).** Censorship-through-noise; dismay operationalized.
17. **Thomas Rid, *Active Measures* (2020).** A century of operations — the best source for compounding, laundering, and forgeries; feeds Tier-1 badge content directly.
18. **Stanley Cohen, *Folk Devils and Moral Panics* (1972).** The phylogeny of the immigrants case — scapegoat narratives as a documented species across centuries.
19. **Benkler, Faris & Roberts, *Network Propaganda* (2018).** Ecosystem-level analysis; why supply-side structure matters more than individual gullibility.
20. **Bowker & Star, *Sorting Things Out: Classification and Its Consequences* (1999).** THE book on what classification systems do to the things they classify — your genus/species worry, treated as a discipline. Read alongside **Lakoff, *Women, Fire, and Dangerous Things*** (prototype categories vs. classical categories — why facets beat trees, from cognitive science).
21. *(For the Smuggled Premise specifically:)* **Lewis, "Scorekeeping in a Language Game" (1979)** and **von Fintel, "What is presupposition accommodation?"** — the formal machinery behind arguing-inside-the-frame.

The three-axis work runs as the standing side-channel guide — the games proceed as before; the turn informs but does not block. Findings from the reading land in TECHNIQUE_CONVERSATIONS.md entries or WHAT_HOW_WHY.md amendments, at whatever pace thought actually takes.
