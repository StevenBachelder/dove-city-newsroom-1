# Game 2 — Curriculum Spine: Anatomy before Physiology
*The intern's lesson. Sequence confirmed against the tagged library, Aug 2026.*

## The governing metaphor (designer's framing, adopted)
**Anatomy first:** naming the parts, in isolation, on clean specimens. **Physiology after:** watching the parts work together in living operations. Physiology is not avoided — it is *earned*.

## The data-driven design fact (from the feasibility probe)
The City News archive contains almost **no clean single-technique posts** (0–3 per technique) — because honest ecology stacks techniques — while it holds **10+ posts carrying three or more techniques** (the Fish Lovers' conspiracy+prenarration+polarization; securitysource's unverified+fearcommerce+impersonation; the miracle-cure sponsored+fearcommerce+impersonation…).
**Consequence:** Term 1 requires **purpose-built teaching specimens** — and the diegesis *wants* this: an intern's course would open with curated textbook plates before touching the real archive. The archive is the cadaver lab; the plates come first.

## The three terms

### Term 1 — The Classic Six (the field's alphabet)
The Cambridge set, honoring the lineage and its published inoculation evidence: **impersonation · emotional language · polarization · conspiracy · discredit · trolling.**
- Taught on **~20 authored specimen plates**: 3–4 clean, unmistakable posts per technique, written fresh (small authoring job; each plate one technique, no stacking, exaggerated just enough to be legible).
- Two honest curatorial notes taught *in-fiction*: (a) **discredit** gets specimens the archive lacks (the accuser-attacking defense post); (b) **trolling** is taught as the historical umbrella that the modern chart splits into engagement-bait + polarization — "names evolve; the field is alive" is itself a lesson.
- Mechanic: name-that-technique, one answer, immediate technique-note feedback (the trap-note voice, reused).

### Term 2 — The Full Chart (the merged fifteen)
The material half enters (manipulated, out-of-context, unverified, hoax — what Cambridge never taught) plus the matched pair (emotional-manipulation ↔ emotional-true) and the Tier-1 outliers.
- Taught on **real archive posts** — the intern now handles the desk's actual back catalogue, with **dominant-label questions**: "several things are happening here; which is load-bearing?"
- **Genuine is always on the answer sheet** — the no-pathology finding; an anatomy course that finds disease in every slide teaches hypochondria.
- Distractor design borrowed from anatomy exams: wrong options are *adjacent* techniques (insinuation vs. unverified; astroturf vs. amplification) — the discriminations that build expertise.

### Term 3 — Compound Anatomy (the bridge to physiology)
The 3+ stacks, dissected: identify *all* techniques in one post, then the load-bearing one, then the **repertoire questions** the sender census makes possible ("across three cases, what is RedAlertWatch's signature combination?"). Threads as case studies: the Scapegoat Thread taught as one operation with compounding as its spine. Final exercise: a full campaign dissection — the physiology lesson, arrived at through anatomy, exactly as promised.
