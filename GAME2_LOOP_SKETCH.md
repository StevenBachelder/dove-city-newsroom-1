# Game 2 — first loop sketch (a proposal to push against)

## The core screen (one loop, three terms, rising stakes)
A post fills the desk — plate (Term 1) or archive item with full City News context (Terms 2–3). Below it, **the Chart**: the technique taxonomy as a tappable reference card (the Field Guide's successor — always open, never hidden; anatomy students keep the atlas on the table).
Player action by term:
- **T1:** pick ONE technique → immediate feedback: the plate's *tell*, in the trap-note voice.
- **T2:** pick the **dominant** technique (genuine always present) → feedback = tell + the full label set revealed ("also present: …"), teaching stacks before requiring them.
- **T3:** **multi-select** all present + mark one as load-bearing → scored on precision AND recall; repertoire questions between cases ("what is this sender's signature pair?").

## Scoring (inherits the ecology, not the numbers)
Right = understanding paid in explanation (the tell IS the reward, per principle 3). Near-miss (an adjacent distractor) = partial credit + the *discrimination note* ("insinuation vs unverified: unverified describes the evidence state; insinuation names the method"). Far-miss = the plate re-taught. **No mistake-door in Term 1** — the atlas is a classroom; the door begins in Term 2 (the archive deserves seriousness).

## Ceremony inheritance
Badges are the SAME badges as City News — the intern is earning the senior versions: each Game-1 badge gains a second state ("named in the field"). New techniques (discredit, insinuation, amplification…) mint fresh badges in the same visual family. The Master equivalent: **The Chart itself, completed** — the player's taxonomy, every entry earned by demonstration.

## Four open questions for the designer (the back-and-forth begins here)
1. **Confidence mechanic?** T2 could ask "how sure?" (1–3 pins) — richer data for research, but one more click per post. Worth it?
2. **Wrong-answer cost:** door-and-retries as in Game 1, or softer (anatomy exams allow resits)? My lean: doors in T2–T3 only, gentler than Game 1's.
3. **Plates vs archive interleaving:** strict terms (all plates, then archive) or braided (each new technique: plate → immediately its archive instances)? My lean: braided — anatomy sticks when the cadaver follows the atlas within minutes.
4. **The genuine dilemma:** in T2, five of fifteen answer "genuine." Show that ratio to players ("the city is mostly real — your job is precision") or let them discover it? My lean: tell them at term intro; it frames the course as calibration, not suspicion.
