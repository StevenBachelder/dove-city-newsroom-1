# The Master Taxonomy — manipulation techniques across four sources
*Bad News (Cambridge) · Harmony Square · City News · the nine outliers — merged, deduplicated, and gap-analyzed.*
*Working document for Game 2's badge set. NB: the Bad News / Harmony Square lists below are reconstructed from the published literature — have the Cambridge/Uppsala partners confirm exact wording before anything ships.*

## Part 1 — The four inventories as given

**Bad News (6):** impersonation · emotion (manipulative emotional language) · polarization · conspiracy · discredit (deflect and attack the accuser) · trolling.
**Harmony Square (5):** trolling · emotional language · polarizing audiences · conspiracy · artificial amplification (bots, bought engagement).
**City News (9 + control):** manipulated · out-of-context · unverified · conspiracy · impersonation · polarization · emotional-true · hoax · sponsored — plus *genuine* as the control class.
**Outliers (9):** amplification · insinuation · denialism · fear commerce · pre-narration · compounding · pre-planted alibi · astroturfing · engagement bait.

## Part 2 — Redundancies resolved (the merge)

| Merged concept | Absorbs | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Amplification** | HS artificial amplification + outlier amplification + astroturfing | One family: making one voice sound like many (bots, bought followers, reposting rings, manufactured consensus). BAD DOG's graph and the water photo's "everywhere at once" are its two faces. |
| **Emotional manipulation** | BN emotion + HS emotional language | Kept as a *technique of the sender*. City News's **emotional-true is NOT redundant with it** — it is the same axis seen from the verifier's chair: the inoculation against over-applying the lesson. Keep both; they are a matched pair (technique + its misuse as heuristic). |
| **Impersonation** | BN + CN impersonation + pseudo-official senders | One badge, widened: persons, institutions, *and outlets*. |
| **Conspiracy** | BN + HS + CN + pre-narration | Pre-narration (prophecy-as-template) folds in as conspiracy's *temporal* trick — worth a named sub-move, not a separate badge. |
| **Polarization** | BN + HS + CN | Identical across all three. The Scapegoat Thread adds its compounding dynamic (below), which is distinct. |
| **Engagement bait ⊂ trolling?** | Partly | BN/HS "trolling" = provocation for reaction; engagement bait = *form-level* urgency mechanics (deletion-panic, countdowns). Related but different levers: one farms outrage, one farms clicks. Recommend keeping **engagement bait** and letting "trolling" retire into it plus polarization — "trolling" has aged poorly as a precise term. |
| **Discredit ↔ denialism ↔ insinuation** | Three siblings, keep all three | Discredit = attack the accuser (BAD DOG's "just another stunt" defense). Denialism = blanket "it's all fake" (the storm skeptics; the liar's dividend weaponized). Insinuation = accusation without evidence ("sources claim"). They form the **credibility-warfare family** — the deck already teaches all three. |
| **Manipulated / hoax / out-of-context / unverified** | CN originals, no overlap with BN/HS at all | Notable finding: **the entire evidence-forensics half of City News is absent from the Cambridge games** — they teach the *rhetoric* of disinformation; City News teaches its *material*. The two lineages are complementary halves, which is the strongest argument for the two-game architecture. |

**Merged working set (14):** manipulated · out-of-context · unverified · hoax · impersonation · conspiracy (incl. pre-narration) · polarization · emotional manipulation ↔ emotional-true (pair) · discredit · denialism · insinuation · amplification (incl. astroturf) · engagement bait · fear commerce/sponsored — with **compounding** and **pre-planted seeding** as advanced moves layered on top, and **genuine** as the permanent control class.

## Part 3 — The gaps: destructive techniques in none of the four sources

Ranked by (a) real-world destructiveness, (b) teachability in the post-library format, (c) fit to the newsroom fiction:

**TIER 1 — should be added; the fiction is hungry for them**
1. **Cloned outlets (the Doppelgänger move).** Pixel-faithful fakes of real news brands (the documented Doppelganger operation cloning major European outlets). *The single most on-theme addition possible: a forged City News page publishing what the desk never wrote.* Impersonation's industrial form — and it weaponizes the player's own masthead.
2. **Malinformation / hack-and-leak.** *True* private material, stolen and released with hostile timing and framing (Wardle & Derakhshan's third category — missing from all four lists). Teaches the hardest lesson in the field: authenticity of material ≠ legitimacy of the story. A leaked real document about a fictional official, seeded pre-election, is a complete L3 case.
3. **Cherry-picked data / misleading statistics.** Real numbers, deceptive selection: truncated axes, cropped time windows, absolute-vs-relative confusion. The deck already renders analytics graphs — the infrastructure exists. Protection value enormous; taught by almost no game.
4. **Firehosing.** Volume as the weapon (RAND's "firehose of falsehood"): many versions, no consistency, exhaustion as the goal. Uniquely *playable* — an inbox flood level where the technique IS the level design.

**TIER 1 addendum (from the Technique Conversations):**
5. **The Smuggled Premise.** Engineering debate about X as the delivery vehicle for frame Y — participation itself is the acceptance. Components are canonized (loaded question, presupposition accommodation, Lakoff frames); the *strategy* is under-named. Text-only, age-appropriate, and Game 1 memory becomes the tell. (See TECHNIQUE_CONVERSATIONS.md, Entry 1.)

**TIER 2 — strong candidates, add as the library grows**
5. **Information laundering.** The fringe→blog→"even the papers are asking"→mainstream chain; each hop adds respectability. The sender census could *show* the chain.
6. **Whataboutism / false equivalence.** Deflection by comparison; the reply-section's favorite weapon.
7. **Humor as deniability ("just a joke").** Memetic delivery with a built-in retreat; radicalization's front porch. Needs careful age-appropriate design — but BAD DOG's universe could carry it.
8. **Rage-farming the algorithm.** Engagement bait’s big sibling: content designed for the recommender, not the reader.
9. **The Gallop.** The Gish gallop / Brandolini asymmetry rendered playable: a live-debate format where volume-and-tempo IS the technique, and the counter-lesson is structural — slow formats are the antidote. (Entry 2.) Engagement bait's big sibling: content designed for the recommender, not the reader.

**TIER 3 — name in the field guide, don't badge (yet)**
9. **Coordinated harassment / brigading** (silencing journalists — thematically apt but heavy for the age band; the docs layer can carry it). 10. **Forged documents** (fold into manipulated/hoax as an advanced instance). 11. **Data voids / search gaming** (real but hard to render in the current search fiction). 12. **AI-personalized persuasion** (emerging; revisit each cycle).

## Part 4 — Recommendation for Game 2's badge architecture

- **Foundation set (from Game 1's library, day one):** the merged 14, with amplification, insinuation and denialism promoted first (their teaching material already exists verbatim in trap notes and verdicts).
- **New-content set (Tier 1):** cloned outlets, malinformation, cherry-picked data, firehosing — four new badges, four new post families, each a distinct *kind* of lesson (identity, legitimacy, quantity, volume).
- **Organizing principle for the guide:** group by *what the technique attacks* — the material (manipulated, hoax, cherry-picked), the frame (out-of-context, malinformation), the sender (impersonation, cloned outlets, astroturf), the audience (polarization, emotional, engagement bait, fear commerce), or the truth-system itself (denialism, discredit, firehosing, compounding). Five families the intern can hold in one hand.
- **Standing caveats:** genuine remains a valid answer everywhere; emotional-true remains the guard-rail against the course teaching paranoia; and every addition must pass the Principle-8 test — *enacted in a post, not merely listed.*
