## Entry 1 — The Smuggled Premise (Aug 2026)

**Observation (designer, preserved):** A proposes X to B, framed by Y. If B argues X — rather than rejecting the question or the frame — Y is passively accepted: a world-view transfers through the act of engaging.

**Known names for the parts:** loaded/complex question (*plurium interrogationum*); presupposition accommodation (Lewis; von Fintel) — hearers grant presuppositions by cooperative reflex; framing and frame-negation (Lakoff); agenda-setting's second level; Overton-window mechanics.

**Distinctness verdict:** the *components* are canonized; the **strategy** — engineering public debate about X as a delivery vehicle for Y, where participation itself is the acceptance — is under-named in the disinformation-inoculation literature. The formulation "arguing inside the frame IS the transfer" appears original enough to publish under this project's flag.

**Badge:** **THE SMUGGLED PREMISE** (family: attacks-the-frame). Tier 1 addition — text-only, age-appropriate, enormous protection value.

**Game sketch:** a poll post — "Should InduChem be fined 1M or 5M for the cover-up?" The cover-up never happened (players who did Game 1 *know* this — cross-game memory as the tell). Correct move: name the technique, reject the premise. Verdict line: "The question was the payload. Answering it — even angrily — signs for the delivery."

**Counter-skill taught:** premise-checking before position-taking; the professional's "I reject the question's assumption" as a named, honorable move.

## Entry 2 — The Gallop (Aug 2026)

**Observation (designer):** the temporal advantage of a lie over truth in live debate — the lie is instant; verification takes time the format never grants.

**Known names:** the **Gish gallop** (rapid-fire claims outrunning real-time rebuttal); **Brandolini's law** (the bullshit-asymmetry principle: refutation costs an order of magnitude more than assertion); firehosing is its broadcast-scale sibling; "a lie is halfway round the world before the truth gets its boots on."

**Distinctness verdict:** well-canonized — adopt the established terms; our contribution is the *playable* rendering and the structural counter-lesson.

**Badge:** **THE GALLOP** (family: attacks-the-truth-system). Tier 2 (needs a new post format: a live-debate clip or transcript).

**Game sketch:** a "live debate" transcript post — one speaker fires seven claims in thirty seconds; the fact-boxes for each arrive next day, too late for the audience that mattered. The player's task: not to rebut, but to *count and classify* the claims and name the technique. Verdict line: "The clock was the accomplice. Slow formats are the antidote — which is why this desk publishes tomorrow what it verifies today."

**Counter-skill taught:** recognizing volume-and-tempo as a technique in itself; valuing slow verification without shame; never mistaking "unanswered on stage" for "unanswerable."
