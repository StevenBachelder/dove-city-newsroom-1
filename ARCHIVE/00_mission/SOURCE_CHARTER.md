# SOURCE — the charter
*The umbrella project. City News is its first game; the intern's lesson its second. Recorded Aug 2026, the designer's words preserved.*

> SOURCE is about defending the trust-system itself against erosion. It is about defending the right of free countries with free thinkers to discuss and decide the futures for their countries. It is about access to non-manipulated information to ensure the possibility of an informed and accountable discourse. News stations have a large role in this and deserve our support — and also our expectations — as a vital part of our trust system and our ability to sustain a world view that we can share.

## What this commits the games to
1. **The anti-dismay position.** The deepest threat is not any single lie but induced exhaustion — the surrender of the conviction that checking is worth it. Every game under SOURCE rehearses that conviction as lived practice.
2. **Support AND expectations.** The newsroom is honored as trust infrastructure *and* held to standards — the player is both its defender and its conscience. (City News enacts this literally: you join the desk, and the desk's own footage can be checked, its own archive consulted, its own coverage attacked and defended.)
3. **A shareable world view.** The goal is not agreement on conclusions but a common floor of method — verification as the thing free thinkers can share while disagreeing about everything else.
4. **Discourse, not compliance.** The games train judgment (proportion, ambiguity, reconsideration), never obedience to any source — including the station itself.
